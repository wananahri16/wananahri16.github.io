Biography

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Kyushu University, Japan. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Tokyo in Japan, following my bachelor's and master's degrees from Tongji University, China. Before joining Kyushu University, I worked as a Researcher at the Port and Airport Research Institute (PARI) in Japan and as a Postdoctoral Scholar at Northwestern University in USA. During my postdoctoral work, I also conducted synchrotron X-ray microtomography experiments as a Guest Researcher at the Advanced Photon Source (APS), Argonne National Laboratory.

My research focuses on the multiscale response of granular materials under complex conditions, using experimental and numerical approaches enhanced by artificial intelligence and computer vision. My interests include morphological evolution across scales, particle breakage and its links to localized instability, and the adaptivity of composite geomaterials.
